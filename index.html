21K Reception

An assistant application operated by 21K (21k.agency).

What it does

21K Reception helps the 21K team manage day-to-day communication and scheduling. With your permission, it connects to your Google account to read and organise email, check and arrange calendar events, and find relevant documents, so that requests are routed and answered promptly.

How it uses your Google data

21K Reception only accesses the Google data you explicitly authorise on the Google consent screen, and only to provide the features described above. It does not sell your data or use it for advertising. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.
