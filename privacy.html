Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy explains how 21K Reception ("the app"), operated by 21K ("we", "us"), accesses, uses, stores and shares information, including Google user data.

1. Information we access

When you sign in with Google and grant permission, the app may access the Google data covered by the permissions shown on the consent screen. Depending on what you approve, this can include your name and email address, Gmail messages, Google Calendar events and Google Drive files. The app never accesses data you have not authorised.

2. How we use information

We use this information only to provide and improve the app's user-facing features: reading, organising and drafting replies to email, checking and arranging calendar events, and locating documents relevant to your requests. We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not use it to train generalised artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

3. Sharing

We do not sell Google user data. We do not share it with third parties except where necessary to provide the app's features (for example, the cloud infrastructure that hosts the app), where you have given explicit consent, or where required by law.

4. Storage and security

Data is processed and stored only as long as needed to provide the features you use. Access tokens and any stored data are protected using industry-standard security measures, including encryption in transit.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

21K Reception's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Your choices

You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can also ask us to delete any data we hold about you by emailing us; we will do so within 30 days.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
